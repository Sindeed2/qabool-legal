Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Qabool Cards ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Purchase Information: When you make in-app purchases, transaction data is processed by Apple's App Store (we do not store payment details)
  • Room/Session Data: When using multiplayer features, we temporarily store room codes and participant names via Firebase Realtime Database

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Cards viewed, session progress, and app preferences (stored locally on your device)
  • Device Information: Anonymous device identifiers for purchase verification through RevenueCat

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the App's functionality
  • Process and validate in-app purchases
  • Enable multiplayer room features
  • Track your progress through sessions (stored locally)
  • Provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Most of your data (seen cards, preferences, session progress) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is never transmitted to our servers.

4.2 Cloud Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Realtime Database: Temporarily stores multiplayer room data (deleted after session ends)
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription and purchase verification

4.3 Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With service providers (Firebase, RevenueCat) necessary to operate the App
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your explicit consent

6. Multiplayer Room Data

When you create or join a multiplayer room:

  • Room codes and participant names are temporarily stored in Firebase
  • Room data is deleted when the session ends or after 24 hours of inactivity
  • Other participants can see the name you provide when joining

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (most is stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (clear app data or uninstall the app)
  • Reset your progress through the Settings screen
  • Cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored until you delete the app or reset progress
  • Room Data: Deleted when session ends or after 24 hours
  • Purchase Data: Retained by RevenueCat per their privacy policy

10. Third-Party Services

Our App uses third-party services with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the "Last Updated" date.
